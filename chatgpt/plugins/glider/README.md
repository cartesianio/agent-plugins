# Glider

Find, install, update, pin and remove the Skills your company offers through
Glider, and publish your own, without leaving ChatGPT. Ask Glider in plain
words:

- "What Skills does my company have?" lists every Skill your company has
  released.
- "Is there a Skill for security reviews?" finds the Skills that fit one need.
- "Install security-review" installs one, at its released version or the
  version you name.
- "Update my Skills" updates every installed Skill to the released version,
  leaving pinned Skills alone. Naming a pinned Skill removes its pin.
- "Pin security-review" pins an installed Skill at the version you have, so
  Glider stops offering updates for it.
- "Which company Skills do I have?" shows which you have, at which versions,
  and whether each is current, has an update, is pinned or is no longer
  offered.
- "Remove security-review" gives the steps to remove a Skill on the surface you
  are using.
- "Publish this Skill" publishes a Skill you wrote as a new version, released
  to your whole company at once or held back for testing.
- "Release security-review 1.3.0" releases a version of a Skill you authored to
  everyone in your company, or rolls back to an earlier one.
